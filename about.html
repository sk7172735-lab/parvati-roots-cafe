PARVATI ROOTS

Born in the

Parvati Roots Café is a cozy little café tucked away in the beautiful valley of Kasol, Himachal Pradesh.

We believe that sometimes the best moments are the simplest ones — a warm cup of tea, good food, mountain views and conversations that last a little longer.

Our idea is simple: create a welcoming place where travellers, locals and mountain lovers can sit back, relax and enjoy the vibe of Kasol.
